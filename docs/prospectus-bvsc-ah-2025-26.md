## Part I — General Admission Information

### 1. Preamble & Competent Authority

MAFSU, Nagpur was constituted under the Maharashtra Animal and Fishery Sciences
University Act, 1998 (Maharashtra Act XVII). Admission to the B.V.Sc. & A.H. degree
programme is governed under section 5 (Chapter II) of that Act.

**Executives of the University**

| Role | Name |
|---|---|
| Chancellor (Governor of Maharashtra) | Shri. C. P. Radhakrishnan |
| Pro-Chancellor (Minister of Environment & Climate Change, and Animal Husbandry) | Smt. Pankaja Pradnya Gopinath Munde |
| Vice-Chancellor | Dr. Niteen V. Patil |
| Registrar | Smt. Mona K. Thakur |

**Competent Authority for admission:** The Registrar, MAFSU, Nagpur. The Associate
Deans of each constituent college (Nagpur, Mumbai, Shirwal, Parbhani, Udgir, Akola)
are the responsible officers for effecting admissions at their college. The Competent
Authority's decision is final.

### 2. Definitions

- **Candidate** — an applicant seeking admission to 1st year B.V.Sc. & A.H.
- **Veterinary College** — a college imparting veterinary education per VCI regulations.
- **Intake Capacity** — the number of sanctioned seats.
- **VCI Regulation (2016)** — the MSVE-2016 regulations made by the Veterinary
  Council of India.
- **Professional Year** — a period of minimum 210 instructional days (excluding
  exam days), except the 4th professional year, which has 315 instructional days.
- **Degree Course** — five complete professional years (5½ years total), including a
  compulsory one-year internship, taken after all credits are completed.
- **Internship** — one year of full-time training in veterinary/animal husbandry
  service, including emergency duties and entrepreneurial training.
- **NEET-UG-2025 Qualifying Score** — the minimum score in the National Eligibility
  cum Entrance Test conducted by the National Testing Agency.
- **Agriculturist** — a person whose main income is from personal cultivation of
  agricultural land they own, within the economic land-holding limit for their region.
- **Constituent Colleges** — the veterinary colleges at Nagpur, Mumbai, Shirwal,
  Parbhani, Udgir, and Akola.
- **Counsellor** — an academic officer assigned to guide a batch of students.
- **Qualifying Examination** — Higher Secondary (10+2) or an equivalent examination.

### 3. Eligibility / Selection Criteria

1. Passed XIIth (10+2) or equivalent with **minimum 50% marks** (Unreserved) or
   **47.5% marks** (Reserved category) in Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology
   and English taken together.
2. Admission is based on **NEET-UG-2025** qualifying score/marks.
3. Candidate must be **17 years of age on or before 31/12/2025** — i.e. born on or
   before **1 January 2009**.
4. **English** is the medium of instruction and examination.

**Notes:**
- Qualifying exam must be XII Std. (CBSE/ISC/state board or recognized equivalent)
  with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology (with practicals) and English.
- Indian Dairy Diploma is **not** considered equivalent to XIIth/HSSC.

### 8. NRI / FN / PIO / OCI Candidates

- 10 seats total, university-wide, over and above intake capacity; no seats reserved
  specifically for "NRI-sponsored" candidates.
- Age: 17 years completed on/before 31 Dec 2025. Minimum 50% marks in PCB/Biotech +
  English at qualifying exam.
- India-schooled NRI/FN/PIO/OCI candidates must have NEET-UG-2025; those schooled
  abroad are exempt from NEET.
- Medium of instruction: English only.
- Personal presence with all original documents on admission day is compulsory.
- **NRI** = Indian citizen residing outside India >182 days/year. Needs an NRI-status
  certificate from the relevant Indian Embassy/Consulate. **OCI/PIO** candidates need
  their own proof of status.
- Fees are the regular Annexure III-A fee **plus** a special fee — see
  [Fee Structure](#2-annexure-iii--fee-structure).
- Special fee is **never refunded**, under any circumstance.

### 9. Admission Procedure

1. Read the prospectus, apply online, pay fees, fill the application form, upload
   documents.
2. Applications are scrutinized; candidates with deficient/wrong documents are listed
   on the website and get a fixed window to re-upload — no new documents accepted
   after that window closes.
3. **Provisional merit list** published; a candidate may raise an online **grievance**
   (fee: **Rs. 200**) against it before the deadline. Grievance committee decides;
   decision is final. If reserved-category documents get rejected, the candidate may
   still contest as Unreserved by paying the Rs. 300 fee difference alongside the
   grievance.
4. Merit is by NEET-UG-2025 score. **Tie-break order**: (1) higher XII aggregate %,
   (2) higher XII Biology marks, (3) higher XII Chemistry marks, (4) higher SSC
   aggregate %, (5) candidate age (older wins).
5. **Final merit list** published.
6. **Admission rounds** — four in total:
   - **First Round**: Region Quota, then State Quota, allocation lists published;
     allotted candidates must report with the allotment letter, originals, and fees
     within the stipulated window or lose their seat for later rounds (unless it was
     their 2nd+ preference, in which case they stay in contention).
   - **Second Round**: fills seats still vacant after Round 1, via a 6-step process —
     (I) fill reserved/unreserved vacancies separately, (II) convert unfilled female
     seats to male within category, (III) convert horizontal-reservation vacancies into
     the general reserved/unreserved pool, (IV) fill reserved vacancies with SBC
     candidates (max 2%), (V) fill remaining reserved vacancies inter-se within reserved
     categories, (VI) fill anything still open by common state merit.
   - **Third Round**: Region + State quota vacancies are combined and filled together;
     no-shows are replaced from the category waiting list by merit.
   - **Special Round**: fills whatever remains after the third round, including any
     unused NRI/FN/PIO seats (which then open to state-merit candidates who pay the
     Rs. 5,000 option fee, then full NRI fees if selected).
7. Region-Quota-round admission bars a candidate from State-Quota admission to a
   college in their *own* region in the same cycle.
8. Post-admission: submit Aadhaar, bank account details, and Voter Card proof to the
   Associate Dean.

## Part II — Academic Information

### 1. System of Education

Total course: **5 complete professional years (5½ years overall)** — 4 years of
coursework + 1 year compulsory internship. Follows VCI's MSVE-2016 minimum standards.
Professional years I–III: ≥210 instructional days each (excluding exams). Year IV:
315 instructional days. Internship: 12 calendar months.

### 2. Registration

- After the allotment letter, the candidate registers with the Associate Dean for 1st
  professional year.
- Registration forms need signatures from the student, counsellor, and teachers within
  the prescribed window — missed deadline cancels the admission.
- No in-absentia registration.

### 3. Subjects & Curriculum

18 core subjects per VCI's revised MSVE-2016 regulations (Veterinary Anatomy,
Physiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology & Toxicology, Parasitology, Microbiology,
Pathology, Public Health & Epidemiology, Animal Nutrition, Genetics & Breeding,
Livestock Production Management, Livestock Products Technology, Gynaecology &
Obstetrics, Surgery & Radiology, Medicine, Extension Education, Clinical Practices,
Farm Practices).

**Credit distribution (81 credits minimum: 50 theory + 31 practical):**

| Professional year | Theory | Practical | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| First (1 year) | 12 | 6 | 18 |
| Second (1 year) | 15 | 7 | 22 |
| Third (1 year) | 15 | 9 | 24 |
| Fourth (1½ years) | 8 | 9 | 17 |
| **Total** | **50** | **31** | **81** |

- Compulsory non-credit course in Year 1: "Democracy, Election and Good Governance" —
  must pass to be promoted to Year 2.
- Compulsory non-credit (0+1) activity, every year except the 4th: NCC / Remount
  Veterinary Squadron / Equestrian / NSS / Sports & Games.
- After passing Year 4, students undergo a compulsory 12-month rotating internship.
- Students must personally purchase safety gear: apron, gum boots, dungaree,
  stethoscope, thermometer, etc.

### 4. Attendance

**Minimum 75% attendance** required in theory and practicals *separately* — falling
short in either bars the student from that year's annual exam (including
compartmental).

### 5. Hostel Accommodation

Available for a limited number of students; **not guaranteed** to every admitted
student, and not a right.

### 6. Discipline & Ragging

- Misconduct/unfair-means cases are handled under MAFSU's 2002 Discipline & Hostel
  Rules.
- Ragging, bullying, or kidnapping ⇒ immediate suspension from college and hostel by
  the Associate Dean, and potential expulsion by the Vice-Chancellor.
- Governed additionally by the Maharashtra Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1999 (criminal
  penalties possible) and UGC's 2009 anti-ragging regulations.
- New students and a parent/guardian must each submit a signed anti-ragging affidavit
  (Annexures VIII & IX) to their Associate Dean.

### 7. Academic Regulations

- **Migration/transfer between veterinary colleges**: allowed only after passing 1st
  year, applied for within 1 month of the receiving college's 2nd-year session start;
  never mid-year. Capped at 5% of a college's intake per year. Registration fees are
  non-refundable on transfer. NRI/FN/PIO/OCI, J&K/Ladakh, and Goa-quota students are
  **not** eligible for transfer; nor are students who failed Year 1 on their first
  attempt.
- **Medical exam**: compulsory for all 1st-year admits, within 6 months of starting.
  Found unfit ⇒ discontinued, no fee refund.
- **Maximum duration**: degree must be completed within **9 academic years**
  (excluding internship) from admission.

### 4. Annexure XIII — Application Flow

1. Read the MAFSU website/newspaper advertisement; confirm eligibility.
2. Visit www.mafsu.ac.in, select B.V.Sc. & A.H., read the prospectus.
3. Register as a "New User"; pay the application fee online.
4. Fill the application form (can be done in stages), upload documents/photo/signature.
5. Preview and submit before the deadline; receive an email/SMS confirmation.
6. Deficient/wrong documents get listed on the site; re-upload within the given window.
7. Provisional merit list published; grievance (with fee) can be raised online if
   needed — no documents accepted alongside a grievance.
8. Final merit list published after grievance review.
9. Candidate fills a preference form each admission round.
10. Seat allotted → email/SMS notice → print provisional allotment letter.
11. Visit the allotted college in person with the allotment letter, original
    documents, self-attested photocopies, and applicable fees.
12. After document verification and required undertakings, pay the college fee and
    receive the final admission letter.

*(If a confirmation email isn't in the inbox, check Spam/Junk.)*
